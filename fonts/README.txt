Deck Sans contains glyph subsets of Noto Sans CJK SC Regular and Bold.
Upstream: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included in NOTO-LICENSE.txt.
The subsets include the presentation text and slide controls, retaining the previous glyph set.
This revision uses the SC faces (index 2) from the distribution-provided Noto Sans CJK TTC files.
Other characters fall back to the operating system's Chinese sans-serif font.
